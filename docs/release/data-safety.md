# Play Data safety form: proposed answers (BORRADOR)

> **BORRADOR.** Proposed answers for the Data safety section of Play Console, derived from what the app stores today. They are not legal advice and are not final: the maintainer confirms each answer, and the legal review of issue #106 validates them together with the privacy policy ([`privacy-policy.es.md`](privacy-policy.es.md)). Play requires the form to stay accurate, so update it whenever the collected data changes ([`docs/RELEASING.md`](../RELEASING.md#google-play)). Contains no real names of schools, federations, or people.

**When Play asks for it.** Not for the first Internal testing release: Play exempts apps that are only on internal testing tracks from the Data safety section ([Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334)). It is required, accurate to the build, before the closed test ([`docs/RELEASING.md`](../RELEASING.md#what-play-asks-track-by-track)). The gaps found when reviewing this draft on 2026-11-16 against the app and migrations at `main` are marked **(review 2026-11-16)**.

Evidence is the repository at the time of writing: `supabase/migrations/` (tables), `powersync/sync-config.yaml` (what syncs to a device), `src/modules/*` (what each screen collects) and `src/core/*`. Re-check it against the code before submitting.

## What the app collects

Everything is entered by the user or captured by the timing screens. There is no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK in `package.json`, no location or contacts permission, and no code that reads the device advertising ID. The individual stopwatch can record video with the camera and the microphone and saves it to the phone's gallery (#1088, merged in #1138): see [Video from the individual stopwatch](#video-from-the-individual-stopwatch). **PENDIENTE de revisión legal:** every text about video, camera, microphone and gallery storage in this document.

Local copy: `src/core/sync` keeps the rows a user may read in an **encrypted** on-device database (SQLCipher, `ENCRYPTED_DB_FILENAME`; the key is generated on the device and kept with `expo-secure-store`, see `src/core/sync/databaseKey.ts` and `db.ts`) so the app works offline. `powersync/sync-config.yaml` limits each device to the rows its signed-in user could read online.

Videos recorded with the individual stopwatch are not in this database or in the sync: they stay in the phone's gallery (see [Video from the individual stopwatch](#video-from-the-individual-stopwatch)).

A guardian's device receives only the school times the school shares with that guardian (`measurements.guardian_visible`) and the rows hidden after an authorization withdrawal; when sharing is switched off, the stream stops matching those rows and the device drops them on its next sync. An adult athlete's device receives all their own.

A coach whose membership is deactivated stops receiving the data of that school: the streams drop the school's athletes, results, plans and assignments, and only the coach's own membership row (with its deactivation time) keeps syncing so the app can show it. In a local run (PowerSync service 1.21.0, `@powersync/node` 1.2.0, one row of each kind in the school) the device of a connected coach went from 1 to 0 rows of that school in `athletes`, `measurements`, `training_plans` and `schools` about half a second after the deactivation, and a device that was offline kept all of them until it reconnected and dropped them on that first sync. SQLite does not erase the freed space of deleted rows, so bytes of them can stay in the database file until it is overwritten; in the app that file is encrypted.
A device that never connects again keeps its encrypted copy until it does. The deactivated coach's uploads still waiting on the device keep being rejected with `42501` into `upload_failures` and do not block the queue.

Handing data to a service provider that processes it on the developer's behalf (hosting, email delivery) is **not** sharing in Play's definition; confirm each provider against that definition.

### Video from the individual stopwatch

The individual stopwatch of a guardian or adult athlete ("Mis tiempos") can record the swim on video (#1088, merged in #1138). This is what the code does at `main` on 2026-11-17; each line names where to check it. **PENDIENTE de revisión legal** for every sentence of this section.

1. **When the camera and the microphone are used.** Only on the video screen, which opens when the person presses "Grabar" (`stopwatch.record_hint` in `src/modules/family-timing/i18n/es-CO.json`: "Abre la cámara con el cronómetro"). There the app asks for the permissions (`VideoStopwatchContainer.tsx`, the effect that calls `camera.requestPermissions`; `videoCamera.tsx`, `requestPermissions`) and shows the live image. The camera and the microphone record only while a recording runs, which starts and stops with its own button, or a few seconds after the clock stops (the person sets that in "Ajustes"). The microphone is used only if the setting "audio" is on (default on: `src/core/preferences/preferences.ts`, `defaultVideoRecording.audio`) and the permission was granted (`VideoRecorderView.kt`, `withAudioEnabled`). The app never records in the background (leaving the app cancels the take) and does not list or open the person's photos and videos: `expo-media-library` is used only in `videoCamera.tsx`, to create the asset and to look up and create its own album by name.
2. **Where the video stays.** While recording, in a temporary file in the app's private cache (`VideoRecorderView.kt`: `context.cacheDir`). When the recording ends, the app moves it to the gallery album **"Swimcro"** (`VIDEO_ALBUM` in `videoCamera.tsx`; at `d46041b` the album is still "TrailSkill", and the rename to "Swimcro" is #1169, which merges before this document) and deletes the cache copy. If the gallery refuses the file, the error is ignored (`keepVideo` in `VideoStopwatchContainer.tsx`): the time stays, the video is lost, and the renamed file stays in the cache until the system clears it. That album is **shared storage of the phone**: any app the person allows to read photos and videos can read it. The video can show minors.
3. **Not encrypted.** The encrypted on-device database (SQLCipher, see "Local copy" above) holds rows, not videos. The video in the gallery has no encryption added by the app.
4. **Never uploaded or shared.** No table, sync stream, storage bucket, or function handles video: `rg -i video supabase/migrations supabase/functions powersync` finds nothing at `main`. The app does not send the file anywhere, does not copy it into `src/core/sync`, and has no share button for it. A time keeps no link to the video except the file name in the gallery (`videoName` in `VideoStopwatchContainer.tsx`).
5. **How it is deleted.** The person deletes the video from the gallery. The app has no screen to see or delete it. **Discarding a time does not delete its video**: after "Descartar" the video stays in the gallery without a time (`VideoStopwatchContainer.tsx`, `finished`: "A discarded time keeps its video in the gallery, without a time"). Deleting a time from "Mis tiempos" removes the row only (`deleted_at` in `family_timing_measurements`; the server removes it 30 days later). A video recorded without the clock started is deleted by the app (`camera.discard`).

**Permissions the build declares** (to be checked against `npx expo config --type introspect`, plus the manifest of `modules/video-recorder/android/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`, which the build merges):

| Android permission | Declared by | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `android.permission.CAMERA` | `modules/video-recorder` (its `AndroidManifest.xml`) | The live image and the recording of the individual stopwatch with video. Asked when the video screen opens. |
| `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO` | `modules/video-recorder` (same file) | The sound of the video, only if the "audio" setting is on. Asked together with the camera, and only if that setting is on. |
| `android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | `expo-media-library` plugin through `app.config.ts` | Saving the video to the gallery on Android 7 to 9 (`minSdk` 24, the React Native default in `node_modules/react-native/gradle/libs.versions.toml`; the reason is the comment in `app.config.ts`). The library manifests cap it at API 32, but the plugin declares it without a cap: confirm what the merged manifest of the Play bundle keeps. |
| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | `expo-haptics` (its library manifest) | Vibration of the timing buttons. |
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | `expo-file-system` (its library manifest) and Expo defaults | Server and sync. |

Not declared, on purpose (#1169): `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` and `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION`: the app only writes to the gallery and never reads it. `app.config.ts` blocks the two the library would add anyway (`android.blockedPermissions`) and passes no granular read permission to the plugin.

`npx expo config --type introspect` lists `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` and `INTERNET` under permissions and the two blocked ones under `blockedPermissions`; it does not show `CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO` (local module manifest) or `VIBRATE` (`expo-haptics`), which are merged when the app is built. The media-library plugin also sets `android:requestLegacyExternalStorage="true"` on the application. Check the merged manifest of the Play bundle before the form is answered. **PENDIENTE de revisión legal.**

### Sharing to review with the maintainer and the legal review

1. **Official results import.** When a guardian or adult athlete uses "Import official results", the server function `supabase/functions/results-import` sends the athlete's first and last name, and the stored identity document number (read back decrypted for the call: `refreshResults` → `provider.fetchResults(identityNumber)`), to the official results source of the organization that is chosen, through the provider adapter `<country>-<organization>` (ADR 0003). That is a transfer of personal data to a third party. It happens only with the `import_official_results` authorization granted by the guardian or the adult athlete (`authorization_purposes`). Proposed answer, confirmed by the maintainer for the identity number and applied to the name too: **shared = Yes, optional, purpose App functionality**, with the same authorization condition. A results platform adapter (`TournamentProvider`, for example `co-colombiaacuatica`, not registered yet) works differently: the person pastes one tournament address, the server downloads that tournament's public LENEX results file and searches it for the identity number (the stored one, or the one typed) on the server, so the number is not sent to the platform. The legal review may refine how the source is described.
2. **Email delivery.** The auth server sends the sign-in code through Resend, an email delivery provider: **confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-11-15; this repository does not show it** (the SMTP host is a redacted variable of the Supabase stack in Dokploy). It receives the recipient address and the code. Treated as a service provider, not sharing. Confirm the provider's terms and where it processes the data. The coach invitation is also sent by email through Resend, the same provider (`supabase/functions/school-console-invite-coach/resendSender.ts`, issue #1046): it receives the invited coach's email address (the recipient) and the school's name (in the subject and the text); the message carries no other data and no link. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` the function sends nothing and only records the invitation (`testSender.ts`, development and tests). Also a service provider, not sharing. The family invitation is not sent by email: it is a link the admin shares by hand **(review 2026-11-16)**.
3. **Third-party sign-in.** Google sign-in is enabled in production (verified by the maintainer in the auth logs: authorize → provider → callback), and the sign-in screen has a Microsoft button too (`src/modules/accounts/components/ProviderOptions.tsx`). Whether Microsoft sign-in works in production is **to be confirmed by Mario**; this draft states neither that it is enabled nor that it is not. With a provider, the email address (and the name, if the provider returns it) comes from that provider through Supabase Auth. It is the same Email and Name already declared above; signing in through a provider is not sharing to a third party.
4. **Sharing inside the platform.** A school can read the times of its own athletes, and a guardian can authorize sharing results with a school (`school_result_sharing`, `share_results_with_school`). That is between users of the same app, enforced by row-level security, not a transfer to a third party.
5. **Share code of an athlete.** Who manages an athlete can give the athlete's code to another family, so that family can send times measured for the athlete (issue #972; the request and its acceptance are #973). Looking a code up shows only the athlete's short name (first name and the initial of the last name, "Ana M.") to the user who typed it; nothing else about the athlete. Giving the code and accepting the request is the consent (rule decided by the maintainer on 14 Nov 2026), and the code can be changed at any time, which stops the old one at once. Sending a time (#973): the receiving family sees the time as it was sent, never the sender's other times; the sender sees only the short name and whether the request is still waiting. Accepted, the time becomes the receiving family's and leaves the sender's list. Between users of the same app, not a transfer to a third party.

6. **Video.** Not shared and not sent to any third party: it never leaves the phone (see [Video from the individual stopwatch](#video-from-the-individual-stopwatch)). An app on the phone that the person allows to read photos and videos can read the gallery album; that is the phone's own permission system, not a transfer by Swimcro. **PENDIENTE de revisión legal.**

## Open questions

- Video of minors: the legal review decides how the policy describes it (section 7 of [`privacy-policy.es.md`](privacy-policy.es.md)) and whether it changes the target-audience answer. **PENDIENTE de revisión legal.**
- Does the video need a way to be deleted from the app? Today the person deletes it from the gallery and discarding a time keeps its video (decision 8 of #1088). Not decided here.
- Does Play treat minors' data held on behalf of a guardian as "data of children"? Depends on the target audience declaration in [`docs/RELEASING.md`](../RELEASING.md#open-decisions).
- Retention: no deletion or expiry rule exists for `measurements`, `authorizations`, or `athletes` today. `results_import_pending.expires_at` is the only expiry in the schema.
- Backups of the server database are out of scope of this draft; they belong to the privacy policy once the hosting decision (ADR 0002) fixes where they live.
