# Role landing and school roles

- Status: the four rules below are **confirmed** by Mario on 2026-11-15.
- Issue: #1093.
- These rules describe product behavior only. The landing is implemented in #1103; the invitation flow and the platform administrator screens are tracked in their own issues.

## 1. Landing by role

Status: **confirmed** (Mario, 2026-11-15).

- (a) A school administrator lands on `/console`.
- (b) A coach lands on `/my-swimmers`.
- Both gain access to timing, series timing, plans, conflicts, and sign out.
- The old Home screen stays only as a fallback, together with the development gallery.

## 2. «Dar de alta nadadores» (registering swimmers)

Status: **confirmed** (Mario, 2026-11-15).

- (c) The school invites the family, and the family creates the profile.
- The school never creates profiles of minors. It only invites.

## 3. Creating schools and school administrators

Status: **confirmed** (Mario, 2026-11-15).

- (d) Creating schools and defining school administrators belongs only to the platform administrator.
